# Neotec Txn Transfer

One-time, zero-footprint transfer of ERPNext v15 transactions from one company to another (same site or a remote site). Install, transfer, verify, purge, uninstall — nothing of the app remains on the site.

Covers Sales Order, Delivery Note, Sales Invoice, Purchase Order, Purchase Receipt, Purchase Invoice and Payment Entry, including returns.

See **RUN_GUIDE.md** for the step-by-step procedure (English / العربية).

Run `python3 verify_tree.py` before pushing.
